# HabitLoop theme music — Suno prompt

## Main prompt

Create an original instrumental theme for a modern habit-building and personal-growth app called HabitLoop. The emotional idea is a small action returning each day and gradually becoming a confident rhythm. Begin intimate and spacious with a warm felt piano motif of four simple notes, soft marimba droplets and a subtle organic pulse like a calm heartbeat. Let the motif loop with tiny variations, adding gentle hand percussion, muted plucked strings, airy synth texture and a restrained rising counter-melody. The music should feel optimistic, focused, emotionally safe and quietly futuristic—never childish, corporate, motivational-cliché or overly meditative.

Structure it as a seamless 75–90 second loop. First 15 seconds: calm arrival and curiosity. Middle: light forward motion suitable for checking habits, insights and community activity. Final section: a soft sense of completion, then resolve naturally into the opening note for gapless looping. Tempo 92 BPM, 4/4, major tonality with subtle suspended chords, warm acoustic/electronic hybrid production, clean mobile-friendly mix, gentle dynamics, no sudden transients.

Instrumental only. No vocals, spoken words, chanting, lyrics, logos or recognizable copyrighted melody. Avoid ukulele advertising music, EDM drops, trap drums, cinematic booms, sad ambient drones, nursery-like bells and aggressive bass.

## Short style field

Warm felt piano, organic electronic, soft marimba, muted plucks, gentle hand percussion, airy synth, optimistic focus, 92 BPM, seamless app loop, instrumental.

## Optional completion sting

Create a separate original 2.5-second HabitLoop completion sound derived from the same four-note motif: soft wooden tap, warm upward two-note chime and a tiny airy bloom. Satisfying but restrained, readable on phone speakers, no harsh high frequencies, no casino/game reward sound.
